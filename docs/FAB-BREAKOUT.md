## Not ready to order

Three GND connections are still open. Each is a minute in pcbnew with the
ratsnest showing.

| Where | What it needs |
|---|---|
| J1 pad A3 @ 50.0, 168.4 | a via to the In1 pour; the GND track 5 mm away at 50.0, 173.5 is the one to join |
| GND pour, F.Cu ↔ B.Cu near 58, 41 | one stitching via in a spot where both surface pours have copper — two island pairs, probably the same via fixes both |

**The 81 `via_dangling` warnings want an eye before ordering.** KiCad raises
these for a via whose only other connection is a pour. Connectivity says the
plane nets are complete — if those vias were doing nothing, the pads they serve
would show as unconnected, and three GND items are all that do. So they are
almost certainly fine, but "almost certainly" is not what you want on a board
you are paying to drill 337 holes in. Open a few in pcbnew and confirm.

Also worth doing while in there: the 5 remaining silkscreen labels sitting on
copper.

## Everything else that has never been checked by a person

This board was generated end to end by scripts in this repo. DRC passing is not
the same as being right, and these are the specific things no one has verified:

1. **There is no schematic, so ERC has never run.** The netlist comes from
   `hw/netlist.py`; its self-checks cover pin names, footprint existence,
   floating supply pins and single-node nets, but nothing checks that the
   *design* is correct — that DIR is the right way round, that the open-drain
   returns go to the right slot pins, that VREF really wants to come from the
   host. Read `build/x68000-breakout.net` before spending money.
2. **The edge-finger geometry is copied from midiori and has never been in a
   slot.** Tab x 44–54, y 47–175, 1 mm lead-in chamfers. If it is wrong, the
   card does not fit and the board is scrap. This is the single biggest risk
   and the main reason to build this board before the real one.
3. **Specify hard gold on the fingers and a 45° bevel**, neither of which is
   default and neither of which is in the gerbers. midiori's ERRATA records a
   fab that would not bevel. Say it in the order notes and check the quote.
4. **The footprints have not been checked against parts you can buy.** The IDC
   headers, the fuse and the DIP-style parts came from the KiCad libraries by
   name, not from a datasheet drawing.
5. **JLCPCB's rotation convention differs from KiCad's** for many footprints,
   and this board has six parts on the bottom side. The CPL will need checking
   against JLC's preview, part by part.
6. **The BOM's LCSC column is empty on purpose.** Assembly matches parts by
   LCSC number against live stock; a guessed number buys the wrong part. Fill
   it from JLC's parts search, and watch for extended-part fees.

## Ordering, once the above is done

1. `hw/build_breakout.sh` then fix the 8 plane vias in pcbnew, re-run DRC.
2. Re-export: `kicad-cli pcb export gerbers --no-protel-ext -o build/fab/` and
   `kicad-cli pcb export drill --format excellon --excellon-separate-th -o build/fab/`.
3. Zip `build/fab/*.gbr` and `*.drl` together.
4. Upload the zip, confirm the layer map and the 4-layer stackup in JLC's viewer.
5. Order notes: **ENIG, hard gold fingers 30 µ", 45° bevel on the finger edge**.
6. Assembly: upload `x68000-breakout-bom.csv` and `x68000-breakout-cpl.csv`,
   fill the LCSC numbers, then check every part's rotation and polarity in the
   preview — especially the six bottom-side translators.
